Why music?

Ask any of our members and they'll tell you: it's the best part of their day.

Friends from day one

Walk into high school already knowing upperclassmen who have your back. Music is a family.

Do it all

97% of our marching members also do another activity. Music fits with sports, theater, clubs and jobs.

Skills for life

Teamwork, discipline, leadership and confidence: the things colleges and employers look for.
