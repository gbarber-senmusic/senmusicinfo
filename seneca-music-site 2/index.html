Our programs

Seven ways to make music at Seneca.

All programs

Marching Band & Color Guard

Our fall competitive marching band and color guard: three-time Yamaha Cup champions and one big, friendly family.

Learn more about Marching Band & Color Guard

Symphonic Band

Our concert band for woodwind, brass and percussion players, performing great music all year long.

Learn more about Symphonic Band

Jazz

Swing, funk, Latin and more. Learn to groove, improvise and play in a tight-knit combo-style group.

Learn more about Jazz

Guitar

From your very first chords to finger-style and reading music, there is a spot for every guitarist.

Learn more about Guitar

Choir

Sing with friends! Choral ensembles for every voice, from classical to contemporary.

Learn more about Choir

Orchestra

Our string ensemble for violin, viola, cello and bass players.

Learn more about Orchestra

Tri-M Music Honor Society

Our chapter of the national music honor society: leadership, service and celebrating music.

Learn more about Tri-M Music Honor Society

Thinking about joining us?

You don't need to be a pro, and you don't have to give up your other activities. Most of our members play a sport, act in the musical or run a club too.

  • New players and experienced musicians are both welcome
  • Instant friends from day one, in every grade
  • Trips, competitions, concerts and a lot of fun
