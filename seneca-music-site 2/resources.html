Every family is welcome. Even an hour or two a season makes a big difference.

Gear guide & upgrades

What to have in your case, and smart upgrades when you're ready. Ask your director before buying anything big!

Everyone

  • Pencil (always!) and a folder or binder for music
  • A reliable music stand for home practice
  • A tuner and metronome (free phone apps work great)
  • Marching season: large water jug, sunscreen, hat, broken-in sneakers

Woodwinds

  • Reeds: keep 3–4 good ones in rotation in a reed case [recommended strength]
  • Cleaning swab and cork grease
  • Upgrade idea: a better mouthpiece and ligature [recommended models]

Brass

  • Valve oil or slide cream, plus tuning-slide grease
  • A mouthpiece brush and snake for monthly cleaning
  • Upgrade idea: a straight mute and practice mute [recommended models]

Percussion

  • Your own pair of concert snare sticks [recommended model]
  • A practice pad
  • Upgrade idea: a stick bag and a set of mallets [recommended models]

Strings & guitar

  • Rosin, a soft cloth and spare strings
  • A clip-on tuner
  • Guitar: picks, a capo and a strap
  • Upgrade idea: a quality bow or a setup at a local shop
