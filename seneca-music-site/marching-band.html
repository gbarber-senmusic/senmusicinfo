Why join marching band?

Now in our 22nd season, we're a loud, fun group that brings energy to Seneca Football and has a long record of competitive success in the USBands circuit.

You don't need experience, and you don't have to give anything up. Our rehearsal schedule works with theatre, athletics and other clubs: 97% of last year's members also did another club, sport or activity at Seneca.
